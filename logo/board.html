What we heard

The brief

A study dashboard for IUT course materials, organized by semester, hosted on GitHub Pages. Built by a student, for classmates. The wordmark and accent already live in the site's masthead; the mark has to survive a 16 px favicon and dark mode.

studiouswarmeditorialfriendly

Success: ownable in the notes/education category, reads cleanly at 16 px, works one-colour and reversed, uses the 45-degree fold grammar shared with the wordmark.

Notes Porbo — identity concepts2
Concept A

Porbo P (chosen)

A bold P whose bowl counter is a dog-eared page — the letter carries the fold; the fold carries the warm accent.

  • Most ownable of the three concepts — no other notes/education brand can claim this P
  • Strongest 16 px survivor: pixel-tested at 16/32/64 and in the live browser tab
  • Second-read fold inside the counter ties symbol to the wordmark's italic accent
